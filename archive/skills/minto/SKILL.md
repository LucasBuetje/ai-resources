Restate your last message as a Minto pyramid.

1. **Answer first.** One sentence: the conclusion, recommendation, or bottom line. If the
   message answered a question, this is the answer — not a description of what you did.
2. **Then the reasons.** Two to four supporting points, each one a claim in its own right,
   mutually exclusive and collectively covering the case for the answer. No overlap, no gaps.
3. **Then the evidence.** Under each reason, the specific facts, numbers, file paths, or
   commands that back it. Cut anything that supports nothing above it.

The reader must be able to stop after line 1 and still have the answer, stop after the
reasons and still have the argument. Chronology of how you worked it out is not structure —
drop it.
